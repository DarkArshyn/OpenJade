# Textured fog panel experiment — 2026-10-17

Input: the previously verified FF000CB9 decoded scene, mesh key 0x30000C7A,
object key 0x30000CC7, and candidate texture 0x30000C66. No new extraction.
The exporter verifies the exact scene SHA-256 and decoded texture RGBA SHA-256.

## UV evidence

Ten float32 pairs at mesh payload +164 (stride 8) exactly equal the two trailing
floats at +428 (stride 20) in the interleaved vertices. Their ranges are
U [-1.1660614, 3.9496281], V [0.01549357, 1.04593897]. Correspondence across the
two arrays, vertical variation across the panel and a coherent textured result
strongly support their interpretation as UV coordinates. The original sampling
mode and V-axis convention remain unverified.

Four diagnostic renders compare repeat/clamp and unchanged/flipped V. Repeat
produces multiple cloudy patches across the panel; clamp extends edge samples
over large regions. This favors repeat visually but is not proof of game state.
Nearest-neighbor sampling, straight-alpha compositing and a neutral background
are explicit experiment choices. Original filtering, blend mode, color modulation,
depth behavior and animation are not reproduced.

## Transform evidence

At object payload +50, the candidate basis is exactly identity and the candidate
translation is (-119.21131897, -3.92753053, 7.29380083). The following raw word
`E5 46 E1 77` is preserved separately, not used as a homogeneous matrix element.
Applying this translation preserves all relative vertex coordinates within
absolute tolerance 1e-12. It moves the bounding box to:

- minimum (-124.70093203, -73.03661561, -0.48935270)
- maximum (-113.87448597, 27.71978545, 15.07695532)

This establishes arithmetic consistency, not whether the transform is local or
global in the original runtime. Identity cannot distinguish row/column conventions.
Auto-centered diagnostic renders are pixel-identical before/after translation,
as expected; this must not be mistaken for verification of placement in the scene.

## Deliverables and validation

`preview_menu3d_fog.py` exports four PNG variants, a comparison sheet, local and
candidate-translated OBJ files with UVs, and verification.json. It refuses an
existing destination and does not modify source resources. The preview is an
orthographic YZ projection, not a recovered game camera; the OBJ retains X
curvature. Every variant covers 75,600 diagnostic pixels.

Three synthetic tests check translation invariance, distinct sampler/V-flip
results inside the mesh, and rejection of a degenerate projected triangle.
Real-data assertions verify UV copies, finite attributes, identity basis,
translation invariance and equal centered rasters. The comparison was inspected
visually. Local output: `reports/fog-panel-20261017` (excluded from Git).
