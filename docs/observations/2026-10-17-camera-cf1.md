## Limits and next evidence

The enclosing encoding is only partially identified. This inspector uses fixed
boundaries for a hash-checked sample; it is not a general animation parser.
It does not decode the prefix, assign semantics to suffix words, establish a
coordinate space, or apply these triplets to Camera02. A plausible-looking float
sequence and a matching count are insufficient to establish runtime behavior.
